# SMHOS Reference Findings

SMHOS uses **Poppins** as its primary UI, body and display family. Its hero title uses a bold weight with tight negative letter spacing, while its navigation is compact, uppercase and widely tracked through text content and layout.

The home-page structure follows a simple full-bleed hero and clear progressive sections: welcome hero and primary actions, online-service callout and events, ministry mandate with pastoral visual, testimonies, ministry/participation cards, a final online-service invitation, and a utility-rich footer. The Great Mandate adaptation should follow this rhythm while retaining its own name, copy, logo, founder portrait, giving details and outreach-led purpose.
