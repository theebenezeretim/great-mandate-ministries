# Recommended Future Website Structure

Keep the current homepage as the ministry’s concise front door. As the ministry develops, move deeper information into focused pages that each support one visitor intent.

| Future page | Primary purpose | Content to move from the homepage |
|---|---|---|
| `/about` | Explain the mandate, story, vision, mission and values. | The full story, Scripture, Vision, Mission and Core Values. |
| `/founder` | Present Pastor Solomon Ufuoma’s background, conviction and ministry leadership. | Founder bio, portrait and expanded ministry calling. |
| `/outreaches` | Showcase active outreach focus areas and future field reports. | Who We Reach, Ministry Arms and verified outreach updates. |
| `/sermons` | Build a searchable library of teachings and future video/audio messages. | Recent Messages preview. |
| `/events` | Publish crusades, outreach dates and event registration. | Event waitlist and confirmed event details. |
| `/gallery` | Present verified ministry photographs and videos as an archive. | The full outreach gallery. |
| `/prayer` | Receive prayer requests and testimonies in a focused pastoral space. | Prayer Request and Testimonies sections. |
| `/partner` | Clarify prayer, volunteering and financial partnership options. | Get Involved form and partnership invitations. |
| `/give` | Make bank details and future online giving options easy to find. | Sow Into The Great Mandate section. |
| `/contact` | Centralise ministry contact channels and general enquiries. | Contact details and message form. |

The first practical split should be **About**, **Outreaches**, **Sermons**, **Events**, **Prayer**, **Give** and **Contact**. This gives the site clean navigation without creating unnecessary depth at the ministry’s current stage.
